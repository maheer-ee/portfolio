# Design System: Maheer Ashhab Naveen Portfolio

## Overview

Home centers Maheer's introduction, with a direct link to Projects. Secondary views use compact editorial lists, with shareable project detail routes.

## Colors

Graphite and warm white form the two canvases. Forest green and beige mark links, actions, and the current dock destination.

## Layout

The shell is capped at 72rem. Home uses a single centered column. Content flows naturally on short screens or at larger text sizes.

## Elevation & Depth

The forest dock uses a soft shadow. Secondary views separate entries with spacing.

## Components

Keep all five dock destinations directly accessible. A beige circle identifies the active destination. Icon labels appear on hover and keyboard focus. A thin divider separates the theme toggle at the right edge; portrait phones and tablets use the device theme.
